Who We Are

A Network Built for Women in Academia

Empowering Women Across the Global South

The Southern Women Academics Network (SWAN) exists to inspire and empower women in academia, aspiring entrants, girls in schools, and young women in tertiary institutions. We create enabling environments for productivity, collaboration, and career growth.

From protected writing time to mentorship and funding support, SWAN is committed to making academia a viable and fulfilling career choice for women.

Four Pillars of Support

Write a Lot

Group writing sessions, daily writing challenges, impromptu hook-ups, writing retreats, and power hours.

Network & Collaborate

Research collaboration, joint funding bids, and book projects that amplify your scholarly voice.

💡

Mentor & Support

Peer mentoring, reverse mentoring, virtual mentoring, and 1:1 mentoring tailored to your journey.

📚

Teach & Learn

Seminars, workshops, and idea incubators that sharpen your skills and expand your thinking.
