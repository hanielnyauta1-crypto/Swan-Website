FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join SWAN?

Women in the Global South pursuing tertiary education from Masters to PhD level, as well as early-career researchers and women in academia.

Is membership free?

Yes, SWAN membership is free. Some specialized retreats or events may have associated costs, but we work to secure funding to support members.

How do I get involved?

Simply fill out the Join Us form, and our team will be in touch with next steps.

Can I partner with SWAN?

Absolutely. We welcome partnerships with institutions, organizations, and funders who share our vision. Contact us to explore collaboration.
