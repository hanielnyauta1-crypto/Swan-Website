What We Do

SWAN provides practical, community-driven support that helps women in academia write more, collaborate deeply, mentor generously, and learn continuously.

Pillar One

Write a Lot

👥

Group Writing Sessions

Join focused, distraction-free writing sessions with fellow women scholars. Accountability and community in every session.

📅

Daily Writing Challenge

Build a consistent writing habit with our daily challenges designed to keep you moving forward on your manuscripts.

⚡

Impromptu Writing Hook-ups

Spontaneous writing meet-ups when inspiration strikes or deadlines loom. Support is always just a call away.

🏕️

Writing Retreats

Immersive retreats that combine deep writing time with rest, reflection, and connection.

⏱️

Power Hour

High-intensity, focused writing sprints that help you make significant progress in a short time.

Pillar Two

Network & Collaborate

🔬

Research Collaboration

Connect with women across disciplines to co-create knowledge and strengthen your research impact.

💰

Joint Funding Bids

Partner with others to develop competitive funding proposals that support ambitious research agendas.

📖

Book Projects

Collaborate on edited volumes, monographs, and other scholarly publications that amplify women's voices.

Pillar Three

Mentor & Support Each Other

🤝

Peer Mentoring

Mutual support among scholars at similar career stages, sharing challenges and solutions.

🔄

Reverse Mentoring

Early-career women share fresh perspectives with senior scholars, fostering mutual growth.

💻

Virtual Mentoring

Mentorship that transcends borders, connecting women across the Global South and beyond.

🎯

1:1 Mentoring

Personalized guidance tailored to your specific academic goals and challenges.

Pillar Four

Teach & Learn from Each Other

🎓

Seminars & Workshops

Expert-led sessions covering research methods, publishing, grant writing, career development, and more.

💡

Idea Incubators

Collaborative spaces where early-stage research ideas are nurtured, refined, and developed into viable projects.

Experience the SWAN Difference

Whether you're writing your first paper or leading a research team, SWAN has a space for you.
